# Research corpus

`research/sources.yaml` is the authoritative source/integration ledger. `bibliography.bib` contains verified paper metadata used by the report. `notes/` holds concise source-specific notes written after reading primary material.

`research/library/` is intentionally gitignored. It may contain local copies of papers/specifications for search/indexing, but third-party documents must not be redistributed casually.

For each method integrated into WaterLARP, pin the upstream revision and record whether scrub.ts uses an adapter, independent reimplementation, reproduction, or citation/inspiration only.
